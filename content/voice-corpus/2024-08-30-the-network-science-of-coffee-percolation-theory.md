# The Network Science of Coffee – Percolation Theory

In celebration of [Tom Hutchcraft winning](https://www.caltech.edu/about/news/tom-hutchcroft-receives-european-mathematical-society-prize) a 2024 European Mathematical Society (EMS) prize

for his revolutionary contributions to probability theory and geometric group theory, in particular to ***percolation theory*** on general graphs, using tools from geometry, operator theory, group theory and functional analysis.

I have made a python script, [Percolatin’](https://github.com/CodeWrangler55/perculatin), to help you craft optimum espresso. It is roughly based on his paper [Pointwise two-point function estimates and a non-pertubative proof of mean-field critical behaviour for long-range percolation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.07276).
