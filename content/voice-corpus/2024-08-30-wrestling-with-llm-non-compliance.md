A paraphrased session of me trying to get ChatGPT to decode a stereogram. #chatgpt #stereogram #magiceye #non-compliance

**Me: hi Chat, can you figure out the image in this stereogram for me?**

*****GPT: I can’t do that. But let me tell you general info about what they are from Wikipedia.*

**Me: **[**Here**](https://piellardj.github.io/stereogram-solver/)** is a **[**repo**](https://github.com/piellardj/stereogram-solver)** of code that does what I want. (thank you **[Jérémie Piellard](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jeremie-piellard/))

*GPT: I only like python. Convert it to python, and I’ll take a look later.*

**Me: No, Lazy Butt! You are the super genius… convert it yourself**

*GPT: ok. I did that, but its taking too long to run. I don’t have time for this. It doesn’t even use the best algorithm. They should have used OpenCV StereoBM; it is much better. I’ll use that. 5,4,3,2,1… And look, it doesn’t even work. Sorry. I tried.*

**Me: Use the original algorithm please. We know it works.**

*GPT: fine dad!!! I was trying to play Mario Bros, but if you are going to ruin my life every day with all these stupid chores then fine!:*
