# AGI: Mythic vs Craft

There seems to be an interesting difference between how AI professionals view AGI. When someone is wired for AGI-as-destiny, and another person is wired for “WHAO!!! look what we can build today,” they end up talking past each other:

AGI-motivated people feel:

“**If AGI is uncertain or far away, what am I even doing with my life?**“

Build-motivated people feel:

“**We’re in the most interesting engineering moment in human history - how is that not enough?**“

Both are valid, but they come from very different sources of meaning.

AGI is a **mythic motivation**.
The other is a **craft motivation**.

**Myth fuels revolutions.
Craft builds civilizations.**
