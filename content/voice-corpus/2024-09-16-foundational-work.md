# Foundation Model

“**[Psychohistory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychohistory_(fictional_science))** is a fictional science in Isaac Asimov's Foundation universe which combines history, sociology, and mathematical statistics to make general predictions about the future behavior of very large groups of people...”  – Wikipedia

Today I read [this preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2409.08717). The authors are experimenting with predicting how people will react on social media to various news events.  Its interesting to see that there are enough people working on this that they have a benchmark to measure against. This is fascinating to me. Can agents be a more powerful prediction tool than something like a voter survey?

Below are some of the references.  I include them so you can see the variety and the names.  I also want to highlight this paper:  [Generative agents: Interactive simulacra of human behavior.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2304.03442)  I comment on it from time to time, and I suspect I will post about it in the future.
