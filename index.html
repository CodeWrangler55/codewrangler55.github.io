CodeWrangler55 Applied AI business systems
First Normal Form

AI tools that turn executive slogans into operating models.

These are small, inspectable demos built around the places AI projects usually fail: workflow readiness, ROI accounting, verification, capacity conversion, and trust controls.

AI Workflow ReadinessScore a process before choosing what to automate, augment, preserve, or redesign. AI Value LedgerConvert AI activity into a P&L-style view of cost, review load, adoption, and avoided rework. Workflow Edge AgentShow how model output becomes operational action only after policy, evidence, and risk verification. AI Capacity SimulatorSeparate productivity theater from capacity that actually reaches the business. AI Trust ControlModel governance as product controls: citations, policy coverage, injection resistance, PII, and overrides.
